# PSEUDO Y – Brief

## Status
Authoritative project brief based on the user's final approved master prompt.

## Primary persona
Senior Controller / Leiter Controlling in a German mid-sized company, plus small Finance teams.

## Positioning
**Die lokale Schutzschicht zwischen Excel und KI.**

PSEUDO Y prepares CSV/XLSX data locally in the browser. It transforms selected columns, previews the result, and exports the transformed dataset. In the MVP, PSEUDO Y does **not** automatically transmit datasets to AI providers.

## Primary CTA
**Lokal ausprobieren**

## Page grammar
**Chaptered editorial with live surface.**

Why: the site needs distinct chapters, a working-surface demo, and a bespoke Y signature moment. A continuous cinematic world would make the workflow less legible.

## Feeling curve
- Hero — calm relevance; large type, whitespace, quiet typewriter.
- Problem — light tension; sensitive fields inside a familiar controller table become visible.
- Demo — curiosity + control; the visitor sees a value change after selecting a rule.
- Y flow — peak / aha; transformed working data and mapping visibly separate.
- Transformations — competence; the methods are grouped by purpose.
- Local-first — trust; the flow ends at local export, not at an AI provider.
- Use cases — recognition; familiar controller questions.
- Pricing — clarity; calm, legible offers.
- Footer — closure; simple next action.

## Peak
> “Jetzt verstehe ich, was mit meinen Originalwerten passiert.”

Lives in the Y signature section.

## Tell-someone sentence
> It’s the site where you see the working dataset continue to export while the original↔pseudonym mapping visibly branches off into a separate path.

## Product boundaries
- No automatic AI-provider transfer in MVP.
- No claim of automatic anonymisation or universal GDPR compliance.
- Mapping storage/export/re-identification must only be marketed when technically implemented.
- CSV/XLSX support must communicate workbook limitations transparently.
