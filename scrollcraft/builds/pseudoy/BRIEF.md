# PSEUDO Y ScrollCraft Brief

Status: Self-authored from explicit project decisions already supplied by the user. No new interview requested because the relevant decisions are already present in the project conversation.

## 1. Vibe

User direction: visually convincing first, editorial, airy, premium, quiet, lots of white, restrained blue, no cyber glow, no generic AI look.

Reference direction already supplied: TESORIN as a visual quality reference. Use the qualities, not the structure: generous whitespace, calm composition, refined typography, premium pacing.

## 2. Scroll journey

1. Opening: immediate visual confidence, almost no cognitive load.
2. Recognition: the visitor sees familiar Controlling data and recognises the sensitivity of original values.
3. Product surface: the actual transformation workflow becomes visible and operable.
4. Peak: the Y physically separates transformed export from the separate mapping path.
5. Trust: local-first flow is shown clearly and calmly.
6. Commercial clarity: pricing is easy to scan without becoming a card wall.
7. Resolve: the close holds on the product promise and one action.

## 3. Energy curve

Hero: calm, high-confidence.
Recognition: slightly tense.
Product surface: curious and controlled.
Y split: strongest visual moment and maximum clarity.
Local-first: quieter again, reassuring.
Pricing: crisp and practical.
Close: resolved, dark, calm.

## 4. Feeling curve and peak

Hero: “Das sieht hochwertig und eigenständig aus.” Caused by typography, whitespace and depth, not visual noise.
Recognition: “Ja, genau solche Daten habe ich.” Caused by realistic Controlling values.
Product: “Ich verstehe sofort, was ich anklicke.” Caused by a real, readable table surface.
Peak: “Jetzt verstehe ich, was mit meinen Originalwerten passiert.” Caused by the Y splitting one route into export and a physically separate mapping route.
Trust: “Ich behalte die Kontrolle.” Caused by a simple local-first dataflow.
Pricing: “Das ist schnell erfassbar.” Caused by asymmetric pricing composition instead of equal cards.
Close: “Das Produkt wirkt fertig gedacht.” Caused by a quiet resolved ending.

Peak act: the Y split.

## 5. Signature move

The Y is not decoration. Scroll grows the data path through the Y. Before the split, original values move as one stream. At the branch, transformed working data continues toward export while original-to-pseudonym information visibly peels away into a separate enclosed mapping area. The two paths never visually recombine.

## 6. Aesthetic range

Premium-minimal with a strong editorial influence, but not luxury-cosplay. White canvas, off-black ink, one cobalt accent, refined serif display plus neutral system sans text.

## 7. Structural choice

Custom grammar: **Editorial workbench**.

Definition: a title-page opening followed by a real working data surface as the visual spine. Chapters hard-cut between white and off-black grounds. One bespoke data-flow peak interrupts the editorial rhythm. The page is neither a fake dashboard nor a filmic one-shot.

Bans:
- no continuous camera flight;
- no giant full-bleed imagery;
- no repeated equal-card grids;
- no glow or gradient text;
- no section number counters;
- no scroll cue;
- no giant sticky traps on mobile;
- no hero that changes document height while typing;
- no image pretending to be the product surface.

Nav: thin, quiet, white, product-like.
Hero: editorial title page with independently moving data planes.
Close: dark resolved plate with one action.

## 8. Existing assets

Existing P|Y / PSEUDO Y logo assets are available, plus earlier product and data-type visuals. For this rebuild, the primary product visuals should be semantic HTML/CSS instead of stretched raster screenshots.

## Product and audience

PSEUDO Y is a local-first browser tool for Controllers and Finance teams in German mid-sized companies. It prepares CSV/XLSX tables for controlled further use by transforming selected columns locally in the browser.

## What the visitor must believe by the end

PSEUDO Y gives me a controlled local step between sensitive Excel data and whatever I choose to do with the exported result afterwards.
